# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Critique code, not people.

## What is expected

- Give feedback on the work, with a reason and, where you can, a suggested fix.
- Accept feedback on your own work the same way. A review is not an accusation.
- Respect that contributors have different levels of experience, different native languages, and
  different amounts of time. "You should already know this" is never a useful review comment.
- Keep discussion in the open — issues and pull requests — so decisions stay findable.

## What is not

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in any venue connected to this project.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments on the basis of gender, gender identity, sexual orientation,
  disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, age, or
  experience level.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or deliberately derailing a review.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention of any kind.

## Scope

This applies everywhere the project happens: issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments,
and any public space where you are representing ToolForge.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer at **minhaz.rbs@gmail.com**. Reports are handled
confidentially and you will get a reply.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues, and pull requests that break
these rules, and may block a contributor whose behaviour continues after a warning. Enforcement is
proportionate: a first lapse usually means a private word, not a ban.

If the report concerns the maintainer, say so in the subject line — it will still be read and
answered.
